## What changed in the model, and why

Every item here was driven by evidence and is documented in `docs/WORKFLOW.md`
and in the code. The football reasoning is in
`.claude/skills/fpl-football-lens/SKILL.md` section 7.

**Consensus weights are now ours 0.35 / Scout 0.40 / Hub 0.25** (Hub raised from
0.15 at his request · it forecasts specific fixtures with stated minutes and is
refreshed by hand).

**The Hub does not get that weight where it cannot see.** A player with no
Premier League record has it excluded from the season blend entirely, and his
per-gameweek cells damped by 0.81 (measured: it runs 23% hotter on them).

**`early_nailedness` is separate from season `minutes`.** Only the former
outranks the match model in the opening-window gate.

**The optimiser scores the window you will actually own the squad for**
(`_window_board` in the draft page), and **optimises the plan rather than a
proxy** (`analytics/opening_plan.py`). A bench points target is a constraint,
not an objective.

**The 1-attacker-per-club rule is OFF by default.** It cost points without
earning them. The toggle is now "Limit to 1 attacker per club", unticked.

**An infeasible solve names its binding constraint**
(`squad_milp.diagnose_infeasible`).
